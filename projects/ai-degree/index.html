Is Artificial Intelligence Methods and Applications Right for Me?

The major brings together programming, math, psychology, and ethics. It could be a good fit if you want to design AI systems that support human decisions and solve problems in areas like research, public service, or business.

Explore College of IST majors

What You’ll Study

Learn how AI systems represent information, draw conclusions, and solve problems. The curriculum also covers machine learning, generative AI, responsible AI, and how to design AI around people’s needs.

Supporting Courses
