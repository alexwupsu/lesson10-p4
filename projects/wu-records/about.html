Welcome to Wu Records.

Wu Records is a small record shop in Philadelphia. We carry folk and indie records, with Noah Kahan featured this month.

You’ll find Busyhead, I Was / I Am, Cape Elizabeth, and both editions of Stick Season in our collection. Have a question about a record? Ask us while you browse.
