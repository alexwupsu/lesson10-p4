A field guide for product designers

UX Research

How do you find out what people need before you build something for them?

From a question to a design decision

Research planning

Start with one question

Pick something you need to learn before deciding what to build. For the campus booking app, ask how students find a room today and what makes them give up.

User interviews

Ask about the last time

Ask, “Can you walk me through the last time you tried to book a study room?” Then follow up when they mention a step that took longer than expected.

A recent example gives you something specific to ask about.

Synthesis

Look for patterns

Group notes about the same problem. If several students check room availability before anything else, that gives you a reason to try showing open times earlier.

Usability testing

Give people a task

Ask students to book a room for four people tomorrow. Watch where they pause and listen to what they expect to happen.

  1. Find a time that works.
  2. Check whether it has a whiteboard.
  3. Reserve it and find the confirmation.

Research limits

Keep track of what you missed

If you only spoke with students who study alone, say so. Talk to students who book as a group before applying your findings to everyone.

Sharing findings

Connect the finding to a change

If students look in several places for the room number after booking, try adding it to the confirmation screen. Retest the task and see where they look first.
