A little about me

Hi, I’m Alexander.

I’m a product designer who likes figuring out how to make complicated things easier to use. Working on real products has taught me a lot. Books have helped me slow down and look at things from different angles too.

Why I’m sharing

Ideas worth passing forward

I’m selling part of my collection to help pay for tuition. It’s hard to let the books go, but finishing my degree matters more to me right now.

I like knowing they might help someone else who’s still finding their way as a designer. I’d rather pass them along than leave them sitting on my shelf.
